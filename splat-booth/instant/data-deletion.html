Delete your Splat Booth data

Effective 24 October 2026

Removing Splat Booth from your Facebook account deletes your saved best score and your leaderboard entries. Photos are never stored, so there is nothing else to delete.

What the game keeps

  • Your best score, saved in Facebook's game data for your account.
  • Your leaderboard scores, one per chat or group you played the game from.

The photo you pick is only drawn on your device while you play. It is never uploaded or saved, so it is already gone when you close the game.

Remove the game on Facebook

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites. On some versions of Facebook it is under Instant Games.
  3. Find Splat Booth and choose Remove.
  4. Confirm. Facebook deletes the game's saved data for your account, including your best score and leaderboard entries.

If you played in a web browser

The browser version keeps your best score only in that browser's storage on your device. Clear the site's data in your browser settings to remove it.

More information

The Splat Booth privacy policy explains what the game handles and why. Facebook handles your account data under its own Privacy Policy.
