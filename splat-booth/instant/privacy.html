Splat Booth Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

The photo you pick never leaves your device. The game keeps only your best score, through Facebook. There are no ads and no tracking.

About the game

Splat Booth is a casual game where you pick a face and throw tomatoes, eggs, pies and slime at it. It is made by an independent developer and runs as a Facebook Instant Game and in a web browser.

Photos you pick

  • When you tap Upload face, the photo is read by your device and drawn in the game's memory only.
  • It is never uploaded to any server, never saved by the game, and never shared with anyone.
  • It is gone as soon as you close the game.
  • The Share score image shows only your score. It never includes the photo.

Information handled through Facebook

When you play on Facebook, Facebook gives the game an ID for you that only works inside this game. The game uses it for:

  • Your best score. Saved in Facebook's game data for your account, so it follows you between devices.
  • The leaderboard. Your score is posted to the game's Facebook leaderboard, where other players can see your rank and score.
  • Sharing. If you tap Share score, Facebook posts the score card where you choose.

The game does not receive your name, email, friends list or profile photo. Facebook handles this information under its own Privacy Policy.

What the game does not do

  • No ads and no in-game purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking cookies or third-party trackers.
  • No selling or renting of any information.

In a web browser, the game keeps your best score in that browser's own storage on your device.

Children

Splat Booth is not directed at children under 13, and the game does not knowingly collect information from them.

Removing your data

To remove your saved best score and leaderboard entry, remove Splat Booth from your Facebook account: open Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Instant Games), choose Splat Booth and remove it. In a web browser, clearing the site's data removes the saved score.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, message the developer through the Splat Booth page on Facebook.

Splat Booth asks players to use only faces they have permission to use.
